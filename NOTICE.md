# Notice

## Fan content

Table Aid is an **unofficial fan tool**. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by,
sponsored by, or specifically approved by Wizards of the Coast LLC.

*Dungeons & Dragons* and *D&D* are trademarks of Wizards of the Coast LLC, used
here only to describe what this tool is for.

This repository reproduces **no official stat blocks, spell text or rules text**.
Every creature, spell description, condition summary and rule paraphrase in it
is written fresh and mechanically equivalent. Where the tracker refers to an
official rule it does so in its own words and points you at your own book.

Keep it that way. If you contribute, do not paste text out of a rulebook — the
project's usefulness and its right to exist both depend on that line.

## Third-party content

None. The tracker is a single HTML file with no libraries, no fonts, no CDN
references and no network calls at runtime. The illustrations in the demo are
vector drawings generated by `build/demo_art.py`; there are no stock images,
no downloaded artwork and no scanned material anywhere in this repository.

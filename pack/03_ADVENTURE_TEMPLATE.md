## WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS IN EACH SCENE

*This is the part that pays off most. For each scene, three to eight things: what the party does,
who they talk to, what you must remember to say, what you're planting for later. Fragments are fine.*

```
SCENE 1 — The hiring
- The clerk asks each of them the same two questions. That's the character introduction.
- Write down their answers — the monster in scene 5 uses them.
- Plant the woman praying in the corner. She's with the cult. They meet her again at 1:05.
- Name three other hirelings out loud so one of them can die later.

SCENE 2 — The town
- Three errands, they pick two. Don't map the town.
- ★ The bookshop: 150gp for the glyph rubbings. This is one of two keys to the vault puzzle.
- Rumour table, roll or pick two.
```

## THE CREATURES

*Anything you know. A name and a vibe is enough — Claude will build the numbers and mark them red
so you can check. The one thing worth giving if you have it is the **Constitution save**, because
the tracker runs concentration checks off it.*

```
Drowned Chorister — CR 3, undead. AC 13, HP 45, speed 20 ft, swim 30 ft.
  STR 14 DEX 12 CON 16 INT 6 WIS 10 CHA 8. Con save +5.
  Multiattack: two slams, +5, 1d8+2 bludgeoning.
  Drowning Song: 30 ft, DC 13 Wis, 2d6 psychic and no reactions for a round.
  AT START OF COMBAT: they're already singing — everyone entering saves immediately.

Sump Warden — CR 8 construct. AC 17, HP 130. Con save +7. Two legendary resistances.
  AT START OF ITS TURN: regains 10 HP if standing in water.
  Recharge 5–6: Pressure Burst, 20 ft, DC 16 Dex, 6d8.

NPC — Ilse Marrant, surveyor, rescuable, badly hurt. CR 1. Speed 20 ft, broken ankle.
  Knows the vault combination, trades it for being carried out.

NPC — the two mercenaries the party can hire. No idea what they are. You pick, CR 4-ish.
```

Say **NPC** for anything the party might rescue, hire, escort or talk to — those get green cards
and are treated as allies everywhere in the tool.

Say **AT START OF COMBAT** or **AT START OF ITS TURN** for anything automatic. Those become pop-ups
that fire at the right moment, which is the difference between remembering regeneration and not.

## MY HOMEBREW RULE

*The thing that makes this campaign yours. Anything with tiers and a saving throw becomes a
resolver that does the arithmetic at the table. Skip this if you don't have one — don't let
Claude invent one to fill the tab.*

```
THE LONG DARK
Every hour underground without a lit flame, everyone makes a Wisdom save.
Tier = hours gone dark.
  1–2 hours: DC 10. Fail = disadvantage on Perception. Success = nothing.
  3–5 hours: DC 14. Fail = 1 exhaustion and roll on Whispers. Success = disadvantage.
  6+ hours:  DC 18. Fail = 2 exhaustion and you hear your own name. Success = 1 exhaustion.

Whispers (d6):
  1 someone calls you from behind   2 your lantern was never lit
  3 you have been here before       4 the walls are breathing
  5 you are alone                   6 you are not
```
